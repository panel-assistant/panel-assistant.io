Panel Firmware Archive is a tool used by the Panel Assistant project to preserve vendor firmware and related technical documentation for wall panels. It helps keep discontinued hardware usable when a vendor removes its downloads or support.

The tool runs on the operator's computer and uses rclone to transfer selected files to and from an authorised Google Drive archive. Google sign-in authorises that desktop tool. Reading this website does not connect your Google account or give the project access to your Drive.

The Google Drive permission covers the authorised account's files. Archive operations are configured for a selected archive folder. Its owner controls which archived files are shared with the community.

Read the [Privacy policy](/firmware-archive/privacy/) and [Terms of service](/firmware-archive/terms/) before authorising access. This tool is intended for project operators using an account they own or are authorised to manage.

See the [firmware guide](/hardware/firmware/) for information about panel firmware.
