Panel Firmware Archive is a desktop tool for the Panel Assistant project's private canonical collection of panel firmware and related technical material. It preserves originals encountered by the project, including material obtained from community or unofficial sources, for provenance checks, hardware analysis and long-term research.

The tool runs on the operator's computer and uses rclone to transfer selected files to and from an authorised Google Drive archive. Google sign-in authorises that desktop tool. Reading this website does not connect your Google account or give the project access to your Drive.

The Google Drive permission covers the authorised account's files. Archive operations are configured for a selected private archive folder. The archive is not a public firmware download service.

The archive owner may privately provide a direct download link for a selected file. Such a link is for the intended recipient's private use and must not be republished.

Read the [Privacy policy](/firmware-archive/privacy/) and [Terms of service](/firmware-archive/terms/) before authorising access. This tool is intended for project operators using an account they own or are authorised to manage.

Public firmware references follow a separate process: the project submits vendor-published release URLs to the Internet Archive and links confirmed captures, which can remain available when the original download is withdrawn. The [firmware guide](/hardware/firmware/) describes that public catalogue. The private Google Drive archive is not its download source.

Contact the project at [privacy@panel-assistant.io](mailto:privacy@panel-assistant.io).
