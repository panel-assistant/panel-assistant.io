Stable follows the Companion's full releases and Pre-release also includes its test builds. The channel only has an effect while [Companion auto-update](#companion_auto_update) is on, and when it is, changing the channel starts an update check straight away instead of waiting for the daily one. A safety ceiling set by the panel's profile still applies on either channel, so Pre-release cannot take a panel past the highest Companion version known to work on it. In Hardened mode, a channel change that would start an install needs approval at the panel.
