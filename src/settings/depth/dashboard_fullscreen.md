With this on, the dashboard fills the whole screen. Android's status bar and navigation bar stay hidden while the built-in renderer is in front, and the panel hides them again after you have revealed them with a swipe or after a dialog brings them back. Because a swipe from an edge always shows them briefly, this cannot shut anyone out of Android. A change applies straight away, without reloading the dashboard.

Turn it off if you want the clock and notifications visible, or use Android's on-screen buttons regularly. It is independent of [Hide Home Assistant navigation (native)](#dashboard_native_kiosk), which changes Home Assistant's own header and sidebar, and of the Android lock, which is what stops someone leaving the app. It has no effect on a separate dashboard app.
