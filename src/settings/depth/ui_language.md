The choices are Automatic, English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Simplified Chinese, Dutch, Polish and Ukrainian. Dutch, Polish and Ukrainian are early-access translations: they are machine drafts that have not yet been independently reviewed, shown so you get your language rather than English.

The setting covers Panel Assistant's own screens and web pages, including the Configure page and the app's native Android screens. It does not change the Home Assistant dashboard, which follows your Home Assistant profile. When you save a new language, the Configure page clears any language previously picked in that browser and reloads so every label and help text is fetched in the new language.
