This is a small update to the panel app, made up of fixes for things that turned up in the first days of 1.0.1. Proximity learning now finishes as soon as the panel has seen the empty room, a screen that did not wake on a tap tries again on the next one, and a WebView that went back to an older version no longer keeps restarting the app. If something misbehaves, please open an issue with the panel model and what you saw.

Pairs with Panel Assistant 1.0.2, the Home Assistant integration.

### Changed

- **The on-panel sign-in names the panel.** Home Assistant's sign-in page on the panel used to say you were giving access to 127.0.0.1, which says nothing about which device is asking. It now shows the panel's own address on your network, and uses 127.0.0.1 only when the panel's web page is closed to the network or the panel has no IPv4 address. The waiting screen for an open setup question now reads "One setup question is still open on this panel", because "in Panel Assistant" read as the Home Assistant integration.

### Fixed

- **Proximity learning finishes without another touch.** In Automatic, the panel learns its proximity sensor's readings for you and your hand from your touches, and for an empty room from steady readings. If you stayed at the panel through your touches and then walked away, it only saw the empty room after the last touch, so presence and wave stayed unavailable until the next touch, and what it had learned about the empty room was lost on a restart. The panel now starts using what it learned as soon as it has seen the empty room, and keeps it across a restart.
- **A tap retries a screen wake that failed.** When the screen had been turned off on purpose, rather than by auto-sleep, and a tap did not wake it, for example while the panel's own screen service still held it off, every later tap was ignored until Home Assistant or a restart woke the panel. The next tap now tries again.
- **A WebView that went back to an older version no longer restarts the app.** If a panel's WebView returned to an older version after an update, the panel app kept reporting the update as still installing and restarted itself about every 30 seconds. It now sees the older WebView, stops restarting and reports the WebView update as available again, so Panel Assistant can offer it.
- **Cancel in Tune the microphone stops cleanly.** Pressing Cancel while Tune the microphone was still starting could let it carry on and start a wake word test, so for up to 30 seconds the panel counted wake words instead of answering them. Cancel now stops it, and the panel answers its wake word straight away.
- **Corrected translations.** 54 translated messages in ten languages now read correctly.
- **Setup carries on by itself.** After you sign in to Home Assistant on the panel, a panel with a setup question still open now goes straight back to its setup wizard instead of stopping on the waiting screen until you tap Set up, and choosing Use this dashboard in the wizard now finishes setup instead of leaving it half done. Back from the wizard still leads to that screen, where Skip stops the wizard coming back.
