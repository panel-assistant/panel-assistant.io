This update is for panels on a separate network, such as a VLAN or an IoT network, where firewall rules sit between the panel and Home Assistant. When the panel loses its connection to Home Assistant, its own Network ADB switch now gives you a way back, and the panel helps Panel Assistant show one discovered card for it. If something misbehaves, please open an issue with the panel model and what you saw.

Pairs with Panel Assistant 1.0.3, the Home Assistant integration.

### New

- **One discovered card with network ADB.** A panel that Home Assistant heard both through Android's network ADB announcement and through the panel app could show two cards under Discovered. The panel app now learns the serial number of its own network ADB announcement and reports it, so Panel Assistant 1.0.3 can close the network ADB card once the panel's own card or entry reports the same serial number.
- **WebView updates without panel internet.** A panel that cannot reach the internet could never update its WebView, because only the panel downloaded it. Panel Assistant 1.0.3 can now download the WebView file and hand it to the panel, which uses it only when its size and checksum match the expected file, and otherwise downloads it itself as before. When a WebView download fails, the panel now says why, for example the file was not found, the name did not resolve, the server could not be reached, the download timed out or the panel was short of space, so Panel Assistant can tell you what to allow.

### Changed

- **The Network ADB switch works after the connection is lost.** On a panel where Panel Assistant keeps network ADB off, the panel kept it off for as long as the app ran, so a panel cut off from Home Assistant, by a new firewall rule for example, had no way back short of a restart. Nothing reopens by itself, but once the panel has been without its connection to Home Assistant for five minutes, the Network ADB switch under System on the panel's own settings page turns network ADB back on, and it stays on until the panel connects to Home Assistant again. This also works when restored settings had network ADB turned on. The switch says when it will work, and Hardened mode still refuses it. The diagnostics page now shows network ADB as off and managed by Panel Assistant, instead of showing Android's USB debugging setting as if ADB were running.
- **Your permission choices are kept on rooted panels.** On a panel whose app has root, Panel Assistant 1.0.3 has the app's helper check permissions after an install or update. The helper turned the camera, microphone and notification permissions, Modify system settings, Display over other apps and Install unknown apps back on even when you had turned them off in Android settings. It now leaves your choices as they are and reports them, so Panel Assistant can list what you turned off in a Repair. Your choices are now also kept when the app starts or the panel restarts: on a rooted panel, the app no longer turns back on a microphone, camera or notification permission you turned off in Android settings, and no longer re-allows modify system settings once you have set it. The app also no longer turns Display over other apps back on for itself after you turn it off: the navigation bar, kiosk lock and camera indicator now grant it only if nobody has chosen a setting, and otherwise go without the overlay.
- **Changes made in Android settings stick.** The panel's screen timeout, boot chime and touch sound settings are now applied once rather than forced back at every restart, so changes made in Android settings stay as you set them.
- **The cannot-reach screen names the address.** When the panel cannot reach Home Assistant, its full-screen message now shows the Home Assistant address it is trying on its own line, followed by the IP address the name resolves to when that is known, so you have something to check against your network rules. The panel also reports this to Panel Assistant as a network problem instead of a problem with its sign-in.
- **No daily GitHub lookup without the Companion app.** The panel asked GitHub for Home Assistant Companion releases 30 seconds after it started and every 24 hours after that, even on a panel without the Companion app. The daily lookup now runs only when a Companion app is installed; refreshing by hand, and the refresh after a Companion install, still ask every time. The diagnostics page shows when the daily lookup last ran and whether it worked, was skipped or failed, and why, so you can see whether your firewall stopped it.
