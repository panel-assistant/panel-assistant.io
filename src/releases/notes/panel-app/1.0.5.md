This update puts network debugging in your hands, helps a new panel find Home Assistant when it cannot use a `.local` name, and supports more panel hardware: the NSPanel Pro Gen2 and the Electron SMT97T with their relays, and the light strip on some Electron WF1589T panels. If something misbehaves, please open an issue with the panel model and what you saw.

Pairs with Panel Assistant 1.0.5, the Home Assistant integration.

### New

- **NSPanel Pro Gen2 relays.** The Gen2 now has its own profile, so its two relays appear in Home Assistant. Thank you to [@cyraxx](https://github.com/cyraxx), who worked out the relay settings, tested them on their own panel and reported that everything else worked too ([android #215](https://github.com/panel-assistant/android/issues/215)).
- **Electron SMT97T relays.** The SMT97T reports the same model as the SMT156 and was set up as one. It now has its own profile, recognised from its build, and its two relays appear in Home Assistant, driven by the app itself without root. Thank you to [@GrisuBerlin](https://github.com/GrisuBerlin), who supplied the panel's details ([#37](https://github.com/panel-assistant/ha-integration/issues/37)).
- **Light strip on some WF1589T panels.** A WF1589T variant with an addressable light strip showed an LED entity that never changed. The app now drives that strip itself: the whole strip shows the colour you pick, at the brightness you set. Thank you to [@GrisuBerlin](https://github.com/GrisuBerlin) again ([#42](https://github.com/panel-assistant/ha-integration/issues/42)).
- **Keep ADB off.** On panels where the app has root, a new setting under System on the panel's own settings page stops the Android Debug Bridge, over the network and over USB, with a plain explanation of why you might want that. Panel Assistant works over the app's own connection and does not need it. The Network ADB switch now works straight away, whatever the panel's connection to Home Assistant, except in Hardened mode. Turning Network ADB on also clears Keep ADB off.
- **Restoring a backup keeps your Keep ADB off choice.** A backup taken while network debugging was on no longer turns it back on: the restore skips that setting and says so in its preview and result.
- **Setup finds Home Assistant at its other local addresses.** With Panel Assistant 1.0.5, a panel being set up is handed Home Assistant's internal URL and each of its local network addresses, not its external or cloud address, and not when Home Assistant serves HTTPS itself. The panel tries each from its own network and keeps the first that answers, so a `.local` name that Android 12 and older cannot resolve, or a container's internal address, no longer stops setup.

### Fixed

- **Wake word straight after a reply.** Saying the same wake word right after a reply was ignored for about two seconds. It now starts a new question. Thank you to [@Ste05](https://github.com/Ste05) for the report ([android #221](https://github.com/panel-assistant/android/issues/221)).
